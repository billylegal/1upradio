Opening The Movement on 1UP Radio…

Rights holders & song removal. 1UP Radio is a non-profit, free music-discovery radio. It does not host, store or distribute any audio files: every song plays through YouTube’s official embedded player, and all music remains the property of its respective artists, copyright holders and labels. If you are an artist, copyright holder, label or authorised representative and would like a song removed from rotation, please contact billylegalmusic@outlook.com with the song title, your name and your relationship to the work. Requests are handled promptly and in good faith.
